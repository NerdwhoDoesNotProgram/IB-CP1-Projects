IB, 2nd period - Crew Shares Pseudocode

GET number of pirates from user

GENERATE a random whole number of units between 500 and 5,000

SET units found to the randomly generated number

UPDATE number of crew members as number of pirates minus 2

CREATE an empty list of crew member names

FOR each crew member from 1 through the number of crew members
    GET the crew member's name from the user
    ADD the name to the list of crew member names

CALCULATE the total number of units already given to the crew as number of crew members multiplied by 3

SUBTRACT the units already given to the crew from the units found

DISPLAY the units found

CALCULATE Yondu's share as 13 percent of the remaining units

ROUND Yondu's share to 2 decimal places

SUBTRACT Yondu's share from the remaining units

CALCULATE Peter's share as 11 percent of the remaining units

ROUND Peter's share to 2 decimal places

SUBTRACT Peter's share from the remaining units

CALCULATE the remaining crew share as the remaining units divided evenly among all pirates

ROUND the remaining crew share to 2 decimal places

UPDATE Yondu's total share to Yondu's share plus the remaining crew share

UPDATE Peter's total share to Peter's share plus the remaining crew share

UPDATE each crew member's total share to the remaining crew share plus 3

DISPLAY Yondu's total share

DISPLAY Peter's total share

FOR each crew member in the list of crew member names
    DISPLAY the crew member's name and their total share
